Care & Longevity

Preserving Your Gemstones

Essential collector habits to safeguard brilliance, natural color, and heirloom settings for decades.

01

Routine Care

Gentle Warm Cleaning

Soak briefly in lukewarm water with mild soap and brush softly behind the pavilion. Reserve ultrasonic baths only for untreated corundum and diamonds.

Pat dry with lint-free cloth
02

Safe Keeping

Separate Pouch Storage

Keep each ring, pendant, and strand in its own velvet-lined slot so harder stones never abrade softer crystal faces or polished gold surfaces.

Fasten clasps before storing
03

Environment

Shield From Heat & UV

Prolonged UV rays, chlorine, and perfume slowly dull organic luster and fade hue-sensitive minerals. Put jewelry on last as your finishing touch.

Avoid sudden temperature shifts
04

Bench Service

Annual Prong Inspection

Everyday knocks loosen claws and wear down basket galleries over time. Schedule a yearly magnification check to keep every stone seated securely.

Complimentary studio checkup
