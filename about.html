Brand History

Our Story

The inspiration behind Gemora and our journey through the world of fine gemstones.

Master Atelier Handcrafted Since 2003

From a Small Workshop to a Trusted Name

Gemora was founded in 2003 by a family of gemologists and artisans with a shared passion for rare and beautiful stones. What began as a small workshop in the heart of the city has grown into a globally recognized name in fine gemstone jewelry.

Our founder, a third-generation gemologist, spent years traveling to remote mines across Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Colombia, and Madagascar, building relationships with local miners and cutters. These relationships became the foundation of our commitment to ethical sourcing and exceptional quality.

Today, every piece of Gemora jewelry carries that same spirit of adventure, craftsmanship, and respect for nature's artistry.

Our Mission

To make the world's most beautiful and ethically sourced gemstones accessible to everyone. We are committed to providing our customers with exceptional quality, transparent pricing, and a personalized experience that celebrates the unique beauty of every stone.

Knowledge & Craft

Gemstone Expertise

Our deep knowledge of precious and semi-precious stones sets us apart.

Precious Stones

Diamond, Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald — we source only the finest quality precious stones, each carefully evaluated for color, clarity, cut, and carat weight.

Semi-Precious Stones

Amethyst, Citrine, Topaz, Garnet, Peridot, and many more — we offer a stunning range of semi-precious stones to suit every style and budget.

Gemological Assessment

Every stone is evaluated by our in-house gemologists using state-of-the-art equipment to ensure authenticity, quality, and value.

Master Cutting & Setting

Our artisans use traditional techniques combined with modern precision to cut and set each stone, maximizing its natural beauty and brilliance.

In-House Gem Lab

Microscopic Clarity & Refractive Index Verification

Before any rough mineral or cut gemstone enters our jewelry catalog, it passes refractometer, spectroscope, and polariscope examinations to confirm geological origin, crystal integrity, and natural color authenticity.

Responsibility

Ethical Sourcing

Responsible sourcing and sustainability practices are at the heart of everything we do.

Conflict-Free

We only source gemstones from conflict-free regions and work directly with responsible miners.

Sustainable Practices

Our mining partners follow strict environmental guidelines to minimize impact on local ecosystems.

Fair Trade

We believe in fair wages and safe working conditions for everyone involved in our supply chain.

Recycled Metals

Our gold and silver are sourced from certified recycled refineries whenever possible.

Sustainable Geology

From Artisanal Mines to Transparent Supply Chains

We partner directly with artisanal mining cooperatives across Sri Lanka, Madagascar, and Colombia. By eliminating opaque intermediaries, we guarantee miners receive above-market fair compensation while enforcing environmental restoration after mineral extraction.
