The Importance

Why Certification Matters

A certificate is more than a document — it is independent proof of what you are buying. It protects your investment, confirms the stone's identity, and discloses any treatments.

01 — Identity

Confirms the True Gemstone

A laboratory report verifies the species and variety of the stone, ruling out synthetics, imitations, and misrepresentation.

02 — Investment

Protects Your Purchase

A certified stone carries documented value. Certificates support insurance claims, resale, and appraisal with objective data.

03 — Transparency

Discloses Treatments

Heating, oiling, and fracture-filling are disclosed in writing — so you know exactly what you are paying for.

04 — Confidence

Peace of Mind

Buying a certified gemstone means buying with certainty — no guesswork, no surprises, no regrets.

Step by Step

Our Certification Process

Every stone passes through a rigorous, documented inspection and quality assessment.

1

Intake & Documentation

Each stone is logged with its origin paperwork, supplier details, and a unique internal reference before entering the lab.

2

Physical Examination

Gemologists inspect the stone under magnification and specialized lighting for inclusions, fractures, and surface characteristics.

3

Instrumental Analysis

Refractometry, spectroscopy, and fluorescence testing confirm the species, detect synthetics, and identify treatments.

4

Grading & Report Issuance

The stone is graded on the 4Cs and origin, then a numbered, tamper-evident certificate is issued and archived.

5

Final Verification & Delivery

A second gemologist independently reviews the report before the certified stone and its documents are released to you.

Questions Answered

Certification FAQs

Common questions and expert assistance about gemstone certification.

A certificate is an independent laboratory's written opinion on a gemstone's identity, grading, and treatments. It proves the stone is what it is described as — and discloses any enhancements — but it is not a monetary appraisal or a warranty.
We work with leading independent laboratories such as GIA, AGS, and reputable regional gemological institutes. The issuing laboratory is always named on the certificate you receive.
Certification does not change the physical stone, but documented grading supports higher resale confidence, easier insurance, and fair market pricing. A certified stone is generally more liquid than an uncertified one.
Use the verification tool above with the report number and security code. Genuine Gemora certificates also carry a holographic seal and a unique QR reference on the document. If anything looks wrong, contact us immediately.
Yes. Our certification desk can arrange submission of your own gemstone to an independent laboratory. Contact us for a quote, turnaround estimate, and shipping instructions.
Lost or damaged documents can be reissued from the laboratory's archive. Contact our certification desk with your report number and proof of purchase and we will arrange a replacement.
